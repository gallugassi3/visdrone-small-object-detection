# VisDrone Small-Object Detection

Training YOLO11n to detect pedestrians and vehicles in drone imagery,
focusing on the core challenge of tiny objects — many under 10 pixels.

> **Status: work in progress.** Baseline training and resolution
> experiments underway. Results, failure analysis, and demo coming.

## Planned structure
- Problem & why small objects are hard
- Dataset: VisDrone, 10 classes, heavy class imbalance
- Baseline results @640 vs higher-resolution training
- Failure analysis
- What I'd do next
